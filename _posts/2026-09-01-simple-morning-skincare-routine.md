You don't need ten products to have healthy skin. Here's a simple morning routine that covers the essentials.

## 1. Cleanse

A gentle, low-pH cleanser removes overnight oil buildup without stripping your skin.

## 2. Treat (optional)

A vitamin C serum or niacinamide can help with brightness and texture — apply to slightly damp skin.

## 3. Moisturize

Lock in hydration with a moisturizer suited to your skin type (gel for oily, cream for dry).

## 4. Sunscreen — the non-negotiable step

SPF 30+ every single day, rain or shine, is the single highest-impact thing you can do for your skin's long-term health.
